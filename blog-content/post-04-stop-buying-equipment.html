Then reality kicks the door down.

Here's what the traditional wrap shop startup actually costs when you stop lying to yourself about the numbers:

  • Wide-format printer (54" or 64"): $35,000 - $55,000

If you're not printing 40+ hours a week, your effective cost-per-square-foot climbs higher and higher. Every single hour it sits idle, you're losing money on a depreciating asset.

Most new wrap shop owners don't hit 40 print hours a week for the first 6-12 months. Some never do. Meanwhile, the monthly payments keep coming like clockwork.
