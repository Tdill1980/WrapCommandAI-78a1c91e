The Day-by-Day Schedule

MONDAY: Sales and Admin Day (The Money Day)

7:00 - 8:00 AM: Review the week ahead. Check install schedule. Confirm customer appointments. Make sure all materials for this week's jobs have arrived or are in transit.

8:00 - 10:00 AM: Follow up on every open quote from last week. Every single one. Call, text, email -- whatever works. Most shops lose 30-40% of their quotes because they never follow up. This two-hour block alone can add $10K/month to your revenue.

10:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Vehicle measurements and consultations. This is when you meet with new customers, measure vehicles, discuss designs. Book 3-4 consultations every Monday.

12:00 - 1:00 PM: Lunch. Actually eat lunch. You need fuel to perform.

1:00 - 3:00 PM: Build and send quotes. You measured vehicles this morning, now quote them this afternoon while the details are fresh. Aim to send 5-7 quotes every Monday.

3:00 - 4:00 PM: Place print orders with WePrintWraps. Submit all files for the upcoming week's jobs. Upload designs, select materials, checkout. At $5.27/sq ft for 3M or Avery Dennison, you know exactly what your cost is going to be. No surprises.

4:00 - 5:00 PM: Admin -- invoicing, bookkeeping, scheduling, email cleanup. Get it all done in one focused block so it does not leak into the rest of your week.

Monday is the day that fuels everything else. If you skip Monday's structure, the whole week falls apart. This is not a day you can wing.

TUESDAY: Design Review and Prep Day

7:00 - 9:00 AM: Review designs with your designer. Go through every active project. Are files print-ready? Are customer proofs approved? If not, push to get approvals today. Every file that is not approved by end of day Tuesday becomes a job that will not install this week.

9:00 - 11:00 AM: Submit any remaining print orders. Check status of orders placed yesterday and earlier in the week. If files need correction, fix and resubmit NOW, not tomorrow. Check current pricing and batch your orders -- remember, free shipping kicks in over $750.

11:00 AM - 12:00 PM: Prep bays for the week's installs. Organize materials that have arrived. Lay out jobs in install order. A clean, organized bay means faster installs -- period.

12:00 - 1:00 PM: Lunch.

1:00 - 5:00 PM: Light install work -- partial wraps, lettering jobs, smaller projects that can be knocked out in an afternoon. This keeps revenue flowing while your bigger jobs wait for prints to arrive.

Tuesday is your bridge day. You are closing out the sales and admin work from Monday and setting up the install-heavy second half of the week. If you can install a small job or two in the afternoon, even better.

WEDNESDAY: Install Day 1 (Full Production Mode)

7:00 AM - 5:00 PM: Install. That is it. Full wraps, big fleet jobs, complex designs. This is your highest-value production day. No phone calls about quotes. No checking email every 20 minutes. Your phone goes on silent and you wrap vehicles.

If you have two installers, you should be running two bays simultaneously. Two full wraps in one day is very achievable with experienced installers and materials that arrive print-ready and on time.

THURSDAY: Install Day 2 (Continue Production)

7:00 AM - 5:00 PM: Continue installs from Wednesday. Finish multi-day wraps. Start new installs if bays are available. This is another pure production day.

4:00 - 5:00 PM: Quick 30-minute check-in on quotes and emails. Follow up on any Monday quotes that have not responded. Send over any new file approvals for next week's work.

FRIDAY: Install Day 3 + Week Close

7:00 AM - 3:00 PM: Final installs for the week. Finish everything. Do final QC on every job before it leaves the shop. Take photos and videos of completed work for social media and your portfolio.

3:00 - 4:00 PM: Customer pick-ups and walkthroughs. Show the customer their wrapped vehicle. Walk them through care instructions. Ask for a Google review while they are standing there grinning at their new wrap. This is the moment of peak excitement -- capture it.

4:00 - 5:00 PM: Week close. Review numbers. How many jobs completed? What is your revenue for the week? What quotes are still pending? What needs to be ordered for next week? Plan your Monday.

That is it. That is the $50K/month schedule. It is not complicated. It is just disciplined.
