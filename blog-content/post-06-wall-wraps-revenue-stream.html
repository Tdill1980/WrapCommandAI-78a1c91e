Frequently Asked Questions About Wall Wrap Printing

What surfaces can wall wrap film be applied to?

Wall wrap film works on most smooth, clean surfaces including painted drywall, glass, smooth concrete, metal panels, and laminate. Textured surfaces like brick or rough stucco may require a primer or overlay first. For best results, make sure the surface is clean, dry, and free of dust or debris before installation.

How does the $3.25/sq ft wall wrap film compare to vehicle wrap materials?

Wall wrap film is specifically formulated for flat interior and exterior surfaces. It uses a different adhesive profile than vehicle wrap films like 3M or Avery Dennison because it doesn't need to conform to compound curves. This is what allows the lower price point -- $3.25/sq ft vs $5.27/sq ft for vehicle-grade films. Both products are printed at the same high quality and backed by our WPW Wrap Quality Guarantee.
