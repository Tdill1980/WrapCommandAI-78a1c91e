The Pain: You Are the Business (And That Is the Problem)

Be honest with yourself for a second. On any given day, how many of these roles are you filling?

  • Sales rep -- answering calls, writing quotes, chasing leads
  • Designer -- building templates, adjusting artwork, proofing files
  • Print operator -- loading media, managing color profiles, cutting panels
  • Laminator -- running overlaminate, trimming, quality checking
  • Installer -- prepping vehicles, applying film, post-heating
  • Project manager -- scheduling installs, tracking timelines, following up
  • Bookkeeper -- invoicing, chasing payments, reconciling expenses
  • Janitor -- yeah, you know

If you checked more than three of those, you do not have a business. You have a job. A really hard, really stressful job that you also happen to own.

And here is the part that nobody wants to hear: your customers do not care how hard you work. They care about results, speed, and quality. That is it. Whether you personally printed the wrap or whether a wholesale partner printed it makes zero difference to the person writing the check.

The Solution: Outsource Print First, Then Build From There

If you are going to start removing yourself from one part of the business, start with print. Here is why.

And here is the thing: printing does not make you money. Installation makes you money. Sales make you money. Printing is a cost center that you have been treating like a profit center because "at least I am not paying someone else to do it."
