The Expenses Nobody Wants to Add Up

The printer itself. That $40,000 printer financed over 5 years comes out to roughly $750/month. Bought it outright? Congratulations, you still need to depreciate it -- that is $667/month in lost asset value whether you acknowledge it or not. And whether you print one wrap or fifty that month, the cost hits. Every. Single. Month.

Ink. A shop running 10-15 full wraps per month burns through $600-$1,000/month in ink. That is if your heads are not clogging and dumping expensive ink during cleaning cycles. Which they are. Every eco-solvent and latex machine does maintenance purges. That ink is going straight down the drain, literally, and it is coming out of your pocket.

Media. You are buying cast vinyl rolls and laminate at retail or small-distributor pricing. Figure $2.50-$3.50/sq ft for the media alone, before a single drop of ink hits it. And you need both print media AND laminate for every single panel.

Lamination. Your laminator needs overlaminate film, occasional bearing replacements, roller cleaning, and periodic repairs. Budget $200-$450/month if you are running any kind of volume at all.

RIP software. That annual license for your color management and RIP software runs $500-$2,000/year depending on the package. That is another $40-$165/month hiding in your overhead.

Waste. Test prints. Nozzle checks. Color-match proofs. Head strikes that destroy a panel halfway through. Media that sat too long and curled at the edges. Rolls that got bumped and dented. Conservative estimate? 10-15% waste factor on everything. Some shops are north of 20% and do not even realize it.

Maintenance and repairs. Printheads alone can cost $500-$2,000 each depending on your machine. Dampers fail. Capping stations wear out. Wiper blades degrade. And when you need a service call from a tech? That is $150/hour plus travel time plus parts. Budget $300-$500/month averaged over the year, because that one catastrophic breakdown in July makes up for the three quiet months before it.

Labor. Someone has to operate that printer. Load media. Monitor prints. Laminate panels. Cut and organize output. Even if that someone is you, your time has a dollar value. At $25-$35/hour, even 20 hours a month of print management is $500-$700. And that is 20 hours you are NOT installing wraps.

Space. Your printer, laminator, cutting area, and media storage eat up 200-400 square feet of your shop. In most markets, that is $400-$800/month in rent being consumed by equipment instead of install bays. Every square foot occupied by printing equipment is a square foot that cannot generate installation revenue.

Electricity. Wide-format printers pull real power, especially eco-solvent and latex machines running constant heat cycles. Add $100-$200/month to your utility bill that would disappear tomorrow if that printer was gone.

Feel that knot forming in your stomach? Good. Because we are about to add it all up.
