Where to Work: You Don't Need a $3K/Month Shop (Yet)

One of the biggest startup costs the gurus push is a dedicated shop space. And yeah, eventually you'll want one. But you don't need it to start making money.

Here are your options in year one:

  • Your garage or driveway -- If you have a 2-car garage, you can wrap vehicles right there. Not glamorous. Absolutely functional. Cost: $0.
  • Mobile installs -- Go to the client. Plenty of installers wrap on-site at the customer's shop, warehouse, or covered parking area. Cost: $0 plus gas.
  • Shared shop space -- Partner with a detail shop, body shop, or mechanic. Use their bay during off-hours. Offer them a referral fee. Cost: $200-$500/month.
  • Short-term bay rental -- Some areas have shared workshop spaces you can rent by the day. Cost: $50-$100/day when you need it.

The point is: you don't need $36,000/year in rent to start. You need a covered space, decent lighting, and a clean floor. Everything else is a luxury you can earn your way into.
