## 1 CAROUSEL — "5 things that make a file print-ready"
1. **Resolution** — 100+ DPI at full wrap size (not your phone screen).
2. **Color** — built in CMYK, not RGB, so colors don't shift.
3. **Bleed** — 0.25" past every edge so there are no white gaps.
4. **Vector or high-res** — logos as vector; photos big and sharp.
5. **Panels & cut paths** — sized to your vehicle, ready to print.
- Last slide: "Not sure? WrapGuru's Check My File tells you free →
  WePrintWraps.com"
